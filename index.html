About

Every project deserves its own sound.

I’m Akashh Roy — a music producer, music director, and audio professional with 8–9 years of experience shaping music for artists, releases, visual projects, and film-related work. My work spans music production, arrangement, background score (BGM), mixing, and mastering, with a focus on translating creative ideas into polished, emotionally engaging sound.

I’ve had the opportunity to contribute to projects associated with T-Series, as well as BGM and film work. I work across two studio spaces: my home studio for production and creative development, and a professional 7.1 studio environment for focused surround listening and audio work. This portfolio brings together selected projects and releases; specific credits and roles can be shared for individual projects where needed.

Whether it’s building a track from scratch, enhancing an artist’s sound, or delivering a final mix and master, my goal is to make every project feel intentional, professional, and true to its identity.
